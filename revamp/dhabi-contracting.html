About

One team from design to hand-over

Dhabi Contracting LLC was established in 1983 as a 'Special Category' contractor in the UAE, built by a team of engineers and support staff with long track records in the country.

The company undertakes diversified turnkey construction projects with in-house capabilities for specialised jobs in civil engineering and electro-mechanical engineering, across industrial, commercial, institutional, high-rise residential, luxury villa and sewerage projects.

It owns a large inventory of construction machinery and equipment, which keeps critical work on its own schedule.

  • 1983Founded in Abu Dhabi as a Special Category contractor
  • 1984Al Ain Luxurious Villas for the Government of Abu Dhabi
  • 2003Abu Dhabi Industrial City, with WS Atkins as consultant
  • 2016Expands into property development
  • 2022Main contractor for Seven Tides' mixed-use JLT project
