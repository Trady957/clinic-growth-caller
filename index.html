Ideal customer profile

Who Claimly AI calls

Privately owned dental clinics first, aesthetic and dermatology clinics second: 1–5 sites, 3–40 staff, private-pay treatments. Gulf incl. UAE, Egypt, Jordan, Morocco, UK and Europe (email first), US and Canada (business landlines). Not hospitals, government or insurer clinics, or groups with their own call centre.

Call first when 3+ are true

  • Google rating 3.5–4.6, reviews mention phones or waiting
  • No online booking or no website
  • No WhatsApp, or slow replies
  • Runs Google or Instagram ads
  • Closed evenings or weekends
  • Arabic market with an English-only site
  • Few or old Google reviews
  • Hiring a receptionist

What to pitch (at most two)

  • AI receptionist (Noor): calls and WhatsApp 24/7, books into their calendar
  • Recalls and confirmations: fewer no-shows, lapsed patients back
  • Marketing: ads, social, Google reviews, landing pages, reported by booked patients

Talk to the owner dentist, clinic or practice manager. Path: call or email → 20-min demo on your calendar → proposal → PayPal pilot payment.
