# Mizan AI Agents: ideal customer profile (ICP)

**Who we sell to:** privately owned dental clinics (first) and aesthetic/dermatology clinics (second) that lose patients to unanswered calls and slow follow-up, in markets we can call: Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Egypt, Jordan, Morocco, the UK and Europe (email first), the US and Canada (business landlines only). **Never the UAE.**

## Firmographics
| | Fit | Not a fit |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Independent clinic or small group | Hospitals, government/insurer clinics, universities, NHS-only practices |
| Size | 1–5 sites, 3–40 staff, 2–10 chairs | 10+ sites with an in-house call centre |
| Revenue | Private-pay or mixed (implants, aligners, whitening, cosmetic) | Fully public-funded |
| Location | Cities above; growing districts with competition | UAE (excluded) |

## Buying signals (score +1 each; call first at 3+)
1. Google rating 3.5–4.6 with reviews that mention phones, waiting, or no reply
2. No online booking on the website, or no website at all
3. No WhatsApp button, or WhatsApp that answers slowly
4. Runs Google or Instagram ads (pays for leads, loses some at the phone)
5. Closed evenings or Fridays/weekends while patients still search
6. Arabic-speaking market with an English-only site (or the reverse)
7. Few or old Google reviews compared with nearby clinics
8. Hiring a receptionist or call-centre agent

## Who to talk to
Owner dentist / founder → clinic or practice manager → marketing manager. Reception is the gatekeeper: ask for "whoever looks after new patients and marketing".

## What we pitch (lead with one, at most two)
| Problem they feel | Offer | Proof in the demo |
|---|---|---|
| Missed calls, after-hours enquiries | **AI receptionist (Noor)** on calls + WhatsApp, 24/7, Arabic/English/French, books into their calendar | Live call with Noor |
| No-shows, lapsed patients | **Recall and confirmation calls**, WhatsApp reminders, next-day aftercare check-ins | Outbound recall demo |
| Ads that don't turn into patients | **Marketing**: Google Ads, Instagram/Snapchat/TikTok, Google reviews, landing pages, reporting by booked patient | Cost-per-patient dashboard |

## Disqualify quickly
No decision-maker reachable in 2 calls · wants a human call centre only · already signed with an AI receptionist in the last 6 months · asks not to be contacted (then never again).

## Path to revenue
Call or email → 20-minute demo booked on Maha's Google Calendar → proposal → PayPal payment for the pilot setup → go-live in 2–4 weeks.
