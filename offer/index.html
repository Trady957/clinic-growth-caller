Where clinics lose new patients

Missed calls & messages

Busy front desks miss calls at peak times and after hours. Each unanswered call is a patient who books with the clinic that picked up.

No online booking

Patients search in the evening and at weekends. If they can't book on the spot, they move on to the next result.

Invisible on Google

Few reviews, a weak rating or no page for the treatment they searched means nearby patients never see you.
